## 信件草稿（英文）

[INSTITUTIONAL LETTERHEAD]

[Date]

To the Publication Fee Assistance Committee
PLOS
Public Library of Science

**Re: Confirmation of the absence of funding for publication fees — manuscript by Lee, Wang and Hsieh, "From fluid shear to sclerostin: a mechanotransduction-resolved multiscale model of bone adaptation, and what it falsifies"**

Dear Committee,

I write in support of the application for publication fee assistance submitted by Dr Ming-Yu Hsieh, of [DEPARTMENT / UNIT], Chung Shan Medical University [/ Hospital], Taichung, Taiwan.

I confirm the following.

**The study received no funding.** The work described in the manuscript was carried out without support from any government agency, research council, foundation, industry sponsor or internal grant. It is a computational study performed by the authors without dedicated project funding.

**No departmental or institutional funds are available for the publication fee.** [DEPARTMENT / UNIT] does not hold a budget line for article processing charges, and I am not able to allocate departmental funds towards this fee.

**Chung Shan Medical University does not hold an institutional agreement with PLOS.** The authors have confirmed with the university that no such agreement or membership exists that would cover or reduce the article processing charge, and the institutional library has no fund for this purpose.

**The only institutional mechanism that could partly offset the cost is a post-publication award.** Chung Shan Medical University Hospital operates a publication incentive scheme that pays authors a fixed sum after an article has appeared. For a journal at this level the award would be approximately NT$20,000 (about US$600), which is roughly one quarter of the fee. This award is not a fund earmarked for article processing charges, is not available at the time of submission, and is contingent on the manuscript being accepted for publication. The authors have disclosed it in their application.

The authors have, to my knowledge, explored the funding sources available to them and have no other means of meeting this charge. I therefore support their request for assistance.

Yours faithfully,

[SIGNATURE]

[NAME], [TITLE]
[DEPARTMENT / UNIT]
Chung Shan Medical University [Hospital]
Taichung, Taiwan

# 表單答案（投稿當下貼上）

**PLOS 沒有可下載的 PFA 表單** —— 申請嵌在投稿系統流程中，且明文不接受以 email 或寫信給編輯的方式申請。以下依 2026-08-04 於 PLOS 投稿系統實際看到的表單題目預先寫好。

## 1. Intent（勾選題）

> *Please confirm you are applying for fee assistance as you cannot afford to pay your publishing fee, and you attest that you have explored the sources of funding listed below.*

☑ **Yes, I am applying for publication fee assistance and the statements I make here are true**

> ⚠️ 勾這一題等於具結「已探詢過所有列出的經費來源」。**2026-08-11 已確認中山醫學大學沒有 PLOS 機構協議**，醫院端只有發表後獎勵金 —— 兩條都查過了，這個具結成立。

## 2. 可用的部分經費（文字欄）

```
Our hospital (Chung Shan Medical University Hospital) operates a post-publication incentive scheme that awards authors a fixed sum after an article has appeared. For a journal at this level the award would be approximately NT$20,000 (about US$600), i.e. roughly one quarter of the publication fee. We note three limitations. The award is made only after publication and is therefore not available at the time of submission; it is a general publication incentive rather than a fund earmarked for article processing charges; and it is contingent on the manuscript being accepted. We disclose it here as the only institutional mechanism that could partly offset the fee.

We have confirmed that Chung Shan Medical University does not hold an institutional agreement or membership with PLOS, and that neither the university library nor our departments hold a budget line for article processing charges. This study received no funding from any government agency, research council, foundation, industry sponsor or internal grant. We have no other institutional, departmental, governmental or grant funding available for this fee.

We therefore apply for partial assistance.
```
