# Scope of the licence

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers the code, the parameter and
validation-target tables, and the documentation in this repository.

It does **not** cover, and this repository does not redistribute, the primary
literature from which parameter values were taken. Those papers remain under
their publishers' copyright. What is version-controlled here is our own
transcription of the published parameter values, with the source cited for
every entry in `data/parameters_literature.csv` and the provenance of each
recorded in [`docs/parameter_provenance.md`](docs/parameter_provenance.md).

This is a purely computational study. No human or animal data were generated
or are contained here, and no institutional review board approval was
required.
